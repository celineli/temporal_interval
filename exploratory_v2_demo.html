Condition 10 (tool effect): A classic mediated-causation display. Square A is the cause, rectangle B is the tool/medium, square C is the patient. The key manipulation: there is ZERO direct contact between A and C — B physically separates them. Tests whether people attribute causation to A even without A–C contact, and how the A–B contact → C-launch delay affects that attribution.
